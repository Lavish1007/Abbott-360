What is your role?

Your role determines which decisions, signals, modules and assistant responses are prioritized throughout Abbott 360.

EL

Executive Leadership Team

Enterprise growth, portfolio performance, strategic risks, critical competitive moves and decision-ready executive signals.

  • Executive decision cockpit
  • Growth & risk priorities
  • Cross-domain strategic synthesis
PP

Product & Portfolio Management Team

Portfolio attractiveness, product gaps, lifecycle opportunities, customer needs, feature benchmarks and innovation priorities.

  • Portfolio & whitespace views
  • Product / feature intelligence
  • VOC-linked opportunity signals
MC

Marketing, Branding & Commercial Excellence Team

Commercial positioning, messaging, customer segments, pricing, reimbursement intelligence and go-to-market performance.

  • Brand & competitor positioning

  • Market & adjacency scouting
  • Strategic competitor moves

Clinical evidence, unmet needs, emerging technology, workflow gaps, innovation signals and research-led opportunity areas.

  • Unmet-need synthesis

  • Prioritize modules & signals

  • Upload research outputs
  • Create commentary & actions

  • Export prototype snapshots
